# Springer Nature journal article LaTeX template (vendored)

**Source:** Official Springer Nature “journal article template package”
(December 2024 / sn-jnl class), downloaded from
https://www.springernature.com/gp/authors/campaigns/latex-author-support
(content id `18782940` / data `v12`).

**Files kept here:** `sn-jnl.cls`, `bst/*.bst`, `sn-article-sample.tex`, `empty.eps`.

For Overleaf / tectonic convenience, `sn-jnl.cls` and the `.bst` files are also
copied one level up into `docs/scientific_data/` next to `scientific_data.tex`.

**Note (Scientific Data policy):** *Scientific Data* does not require or encourage
article templates for submission; house style is applied at production. This
vendored package is used for authoring/review PDF parity with Springer Nature
authoring guidance. At final eJP/Snapp upload, follow the journal’s standalone
`.tex` instructions if requested.
